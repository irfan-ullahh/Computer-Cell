Away from compilers and deadlines, for one evening.

Every year the department welcomes its newest batch and honors the one leaving. Held in the heart of Peshawar with Pakhtun hospitality, the night brings every semester together for music, dining and memories, organized by Computer Cell.

100% alcohol-free

Kahwa bar, spiced chai and mocktails.

Live folk rubab

Pashto strings and student indie sets.

Farewell honors

Shields, batch titles, senior tributes.

All-inclusive pass

Dinner, concert, awards, photo prints.

What the night holds

Royal Peshawari dawat

Chapli kebab, Namak Mandi karahi, dum pukht and kahwa.

Live concert & rubab

Pashto folk, contemporary rock and Urdu acoustic.

Attan circle

The ceremonial dance that unites every batch.

Roast & games

Trivia, senior vs junior showdowns, live stage fun.

Awards & honors

Senior crests, superlatives and tributes.

Photo pavilion

Instant portraits with pakols, turbans and props.
